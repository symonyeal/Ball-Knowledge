# Portrait credits

The bundled photographs below come from Wikimedia Commons. Their licences apply to the photographs separately from the game's MIT code licence. Thumbnails are resized by Commons and displayed with a crop; the original file links provide full resolution, author details and licence history. A portrait identifies the person and may show them at a different age or club from the historical card.

The game shows author and licence credits on each loaded portrait, linked to its original file. Players outside this bundled library can load a photograph on demand from their Wikidata image claim. Those photographs link to their Commons file page for attribution and licence details. Missing or blocked images show a silhouette; the game remains playable offline.
